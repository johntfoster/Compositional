## Why It Matters

- Derives a nonlinear Biot coefficient from a finite-deformation mixture-theory formulation.
- Uses an extended Hamilton principle for a binary solid-fluid mixture.
- Uses a Legendre transformation to express the solid energetic contribution in terms of pressure rather than specific volume.
- Provides a direct comparison point for the current manuscript's finite-deformation poromechanics and Biot-coefficient mixture rules.

## Definitions and Assumptions

- The manuscript focuses on a binary solid-fluid mixture.
- The nonlinear Biot coefficient is interpreted through the local solid specific-volume response to skeleton deformation at fixed pore pressure.
- The small-deformation limit is compared against the classical Biot coefficient.
