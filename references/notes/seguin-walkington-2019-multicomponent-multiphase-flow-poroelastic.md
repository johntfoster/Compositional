## Why It Matters

- Develops an axiomatic continuum model for multicomponent, multiphase porous flow in an elastic medium.
- Uses the Coleman--Noll procedure to derive constitutive restrictions and a reduced entropy inequality.
- Connects the general model to Biot-type poroelasticity and engineering porous-flow formulations.

## Definitions and Assumptions

- The paper distinguishes the solid-medium velocity from phase relative velocities.
- The poroelastic formulation is developed in the current configuration.
- Fluid pressure enters the macroscopic stress-strain response through constitutive assumptions.
