\section{Multicomponent solid entropy inequality}
\label{sec:multicomponent_solid_skeletons}

The variational derivation above allows every phase to carry component mass
fractions.  In this section the solid phases retain that full compositional
structure.  Each \(s\in\mc S\) is a load-bearing multicomponent solid phase with
mass fractions \(\eta_s^\alpha\), while all solid phases share the common
skeleton trajectory
\[
	\mbf x=\bs\chi(\mbf X,t),
	\qquad
	\mbf F=\nabla_{\mbf X}\bs\chi,
	\qquad
	J=\det\mbf F .
\]
The common displacement means that absorbed species, host minerals, hydrate
water cages, and hydrate guest molecules do not receive separate solid
velocities.  Their mechanical effect enters through solid composition,
stress-free swelling, thermal expansion, elastic moduli, and plastic flow
properties.

\subsection{Solid kinematics and state variables}
\label{sec:MC_solid_kinematics}

The solid kinematic split is phase-specific but uses the common skeleton
deformation:
%
\begin{equation}
	\mbf F=\mbf A_s\bar{\mbf F}_s,
	\qquad
	J=a_s\bar J_s,
	\qquad
	a_s=\det\mbf A_s,
	\qquad
	\bar J_s=\det\bar{\mbf F}_s,
	\qquad
	s\in\mc S .
	\label{eq:MC_solid_distension_decomposition}
\end{equation}
%
The distension tensor \(\mbf A_s\) stores pore-space allocation, packing,
stress-free swelling, and reaction-induced accommodation between the common
skeleton motion and the true solid deformation \(\bar{\mbf F}_s\).  The elastic,
plastic, and stress-free thermal/compositional factors are
%
\begin{subequations}
	\label{eq:MC_solid_kinematic_splits}
	\begin{align}
		\mbf A_s
		 & =
		\mbf A_s^e\mbf A_s^p\mbf A_s^\theta,
		\label{eq:MC_solid_distension_split}       \\
		\bar{\mbf F}_s
		 & =
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^p
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta .
		\label{eq:MC_solid_true_split}
	\end{align}
\end{subequations}
%
Here \(\mbf A_s^\theta\) and \(\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta\) are reversible
stress-free maps, and may depend on temperature and solid composition:
%
\begin{equation}
	\mbf A_s^\theta
	=
	\mbf A_s^\theta(\theta,\eta_s^\beta),
	\qquad
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta
	=
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta(\theta,\eta_s^\beta).
	\label{eq:MC_solid_stress_free_maps}
\end{equation}
%
This is where adsorption swelling, desorption shrinkage, hydrate cage volume,
and composition-dependent thermal expansion enter the mechanics.  The plastic
variables \(\mbf A_s^p\) and \(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p\) are internal variables.  The
true-plastic flow is assumed isochoric:
%
\begin{equation}
	\det\bar{\mbf F}_s^p=1,
	\qquad
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^p
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p)^{-1}
		\right]=0,
	\qquad
	s\in\mc S .
	\label{eq:MC_solid_isochoric_plastic_flow}
\end{equation}
%
The dependent elastic arguments are
%
\begin{equation}
	\mbf A_s^e
	=
	\mbf A_s
	(\mbf A_s^\theta)^{-1}
	(\mbf A_s^p)^{-1},
	\qquad
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
	=
	\bar{\mbf F}_s
	(\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta)^{-1}
	(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p)^{-1}.
	\label{eq:MC_solid_elastic_arguments}
\end{equation}
%

The solid component partial densities and mass fractions are
%
\begin{equation}
	\rho_s^\alpha
	=
	\rho_s\eta_s^\alpha
	=
	\phi_s\bar\rho_s\eta_s^\alpha,
	\qquad
	\sum_\alpha\eta_s^\alpha=1,
	\qquad
	\sum_\alpha\dot\eta_s^\alpha=0 .
	\label{eq:MC_solid_component_partial_density}
\end{equation}
%
When component intrinsic densities \(\bar\rho_s^\alpha\) and component solid
volume fractions \(\phi_s^\alpha\) are useful, they satisfy
%
\begin{subequations}
	\label{eq:MC_solid_density_mixture_rules}
	\begin{align}
		\bar\rho_s
		 & =
		\sum_\alpha
		\phi_s^\alpha\bar\rho_s^\alpha,
		\label{eq:MC_solid_intrinsic_density_rule}       \\
		\eta_s^\alpha
		 & =
		\frac{\phi_s^\alpha\bar\rho_s^\alpha}{\bar\rho_s},
		\label{eq:MC_solid_mass_fraction_rule}           \\
		\phi_s^\alpha
		 & =
		\frac{\eta_s^\alpha\bar\rho_s}{\bar\rho_s^\alpha},
		\qquad
		\sum_\alpha\phi_s^\alpha=1 .
		\label{eq:MC_solid_component_volume_fraction_rule}
	\end{align}
\end{subequations}
%

The component material balances give
%
\begin{equation}
	J\phi_s\bar\rho_s\eta_s^\alpha
	=
	\phi_{s0}\bar\rho_{s0}\eta_{s0}^\alpha
	+\mc C_s^\alpha,
	\qquad
	s\in\mc S .
	\label{eq:MC_solid_component_material_mass}
\end{equation}
%
Summing over components gives
%
\begin{equation}
	J\phi_s\bar\rho_s
	=
	\phi_{s0}\bar\rho_{s0}+\mc C_s,
	\qquad
	\mc C_s=\sum_\alpha\mc C_s^\alpha .
	\label{eq:MC_solid_phase_material_mass}
\end{equation}
%
With true local conservation in the solid material,
%
\begin{equation}
	\bar J_s\bar\rho_s=\bar\rho_{s0},
	\label{eq:MC_solid_true_mass_conservation}
\end{equation}
%
\eqref{eq:MC_solid_phase_material_mass} becomes
%
\begin{equation}
	a_s\phi_s\bar\rho_{s0}
	=
	\phi_{s0}\bar\rho_{s0}+\mc C_s .
	\label{eq:MC_solid_distension_mass_relation}
\end{equation}
%
The rate form follows from \(J=a_s\bar J_s\):
%
\begin{equation}
	\frac{\dot a_s}{a_s}
	=
	\nabla_{\mbf x}\cdot\mbf v_s
	+\frac{\dot{\bar\rho}_s}{\bar\rho_s}.
	\label{eq:MC_solid_distension_evolution}
\end{equation}
%
The intrinsic density rate used in the Coleman--Noll collection is obtained
from \eqref{eq:MC_solid_true_split}, the isochoric plastic flow, and the
composition-dependent stress-free true deformation:
%
\begin{align}
	\frac{\dot{\bar\rho}_s}{\bar\rho_s}
	={} &
	-\operatorname{tr}
	\left[
		\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^e
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & -
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		\left(
		\left.
		\p{\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta}{\theta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		\dot\theta
		+
		\sum_\beta
		\left.
		\p{\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta}{\eta_s^\beta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		\dot\eta_s^\beta
		\right)
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta)^{-1}
	\right].
	\label{eq:MC_solid_density_rate}
\end{align}
%

The generic multicomponent solid Helmholtz energy is
%
\begin{equation}
	\psi_s
	=
	\psi_s(
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^e,\mbf A_s^e,
	\bar\rho_s,\eta_s^\beta,\theta),
	\qquad
	s\in\mc S ,
	\label{eq:MC_solid_general_free_energy}
\end{equation}
%
with state set
%
\begin{equation}
	\mc X_s^{\rm mc}
	=
	\{
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^e,\mbf A_s^e,
	\bar\rho_s,\eta_s^\beta,\theta
	\},
	\qquad
	\mc X_f
	=
	\{
	\bar\rho_f,\eta_f^\beta,\theta
	\}.
	\label{eq:MC_state_sets}
\end{equation}
%
No additivity has been imposed.  A source of solid component mass therefore
carries a component insertion free energy \(\widehat\psi_s^\alpha\), defined by
the chosen solid mixture model.  For fluid phases, and later for additive solid
phases, the source-carried free energy reduces to the familiar phase or
component free energy:
%
\begin{equation}
	\widehat\psi_\xi^\alpha
	=
	\begin{cases}
		\psi_f,                         & \xi=f\in\mc F, \\
		\widehat\psi_s^\alpha,          & \xi=s\in\mc S,
	\end{cases}
	\qquad
	\widehat{\mathfrak{s}}_\xi^\alpha
	=
	\begin{cases}
		\mathfrak{s}_f,                         & \xi=f\in\mc F, \\
		\widehat{\mathfrak{s}}_s^\alpha,        & \xi=s\in\mc S.
	\end{cases}
	\label{eq:MC_source_carried_free_energy}
\end{equation}
%

\subsection{Coleman--Noll inequality}
\label{sec:MC_coleman_noll_inequality}

For fluid phases,
%
\begin{equation}
	e_f=\psi_f+\theta\mathfrak{s}_f,
	\qquad
	\psi_f=\psi_f(\bar\rho_f,\eta_f^\beta,\theta).
	\label{eq:MC_fluid_legendre_relation}
\end{equation}
%
For multicomponent solid phases,
%
\begin{equation}
	e_s=\psi_s+\theta\mathfrak{s}_s,
	\qquad
	\psi_s=\psi_s(
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^e,\mbf A_s^e,
	\bar\rho_s,\eta_s^\beta,\theta).
	\label{eq:MC_solid_legendre_relation}
\end{equation}
%
The free-energy rates are
%
\begin{align}
	\sum_{f\in\mc F}\rho_f\dot\psi_f
	={} &
	\sum_{f\in\mc F}
	\rho_f
	\left(
	\left.
	\p{\psi_f}{\bar\rho_f}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_f}
	\dot{\bar\rho}_f
	+
	\sum_\beta
	\left.
	\p{\psi_f}{\eta_f^\beta}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_f}
	\dot\eta_f^\beta
	+
	\left.
	\p{\psi_f}{\theta}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_f}
	\dot\theta
	\right),
	\label{eq:MC_fluid_free_energy_rate}
	\\
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}\rho_s\dot\psi_s
	={} &
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\rho_s
	\left[
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\bar{\mbf F}_s^e}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	:
	\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^e
	+
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\mbf A_s^e}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	:
	\dot{\mbf A}_s^e
	\right.
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & \left.
	+
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\bar\rho_s}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	\dot{\bar\rho}_s
	+
	\sum_\beta
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\eta_s^\beta}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	\dot\eta_s^\beta
	+
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\theta}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	\dot\theta
	\right].
	\label{eq:MC_solid_free_energy_rate}
\end{align}
%
The fluid phases carry no elastic stress:
%
\begin{equation}
	\bs\sigma_f'=\mbf 0,
	\qquad
	f\in\mc F .
	\label{eq:MC_fluid_stress_free}
\end{equation}
%
The interfacial Helmholtz contribution is retained exactly as in the
variational derivation:
%
\begin{equation}
	\psi_\gamma
	=
	\phi\gamma(\{S_f\}_{f\in\mc F},\theta),
	\qquad
	\dot\psi_\gamma
	=
	\sum_{f\in\mc F}\gamma_f\dot\phi_f
	+
	\phi
	\left.
	\p{\gamma}{\theta}
	\right\vert_{\{S_f\}_{f\in\mc F}}
	\dot\theta .
	\label{eq:MC_interfacial_helmholtz_rate}
\end{equation}
%

The phase energy balance is
%
\begin{align}
	\rho_\xi\dot e_\xi
	+
	\sum_\alpha
	\dot c_\xi^\alpha
	\widehat e_\xi^\alpha
	={} &
	\bs\sigma_\xi':\mbf L_\xi
	+
	\phi_\xi\bar p_\xi
	\frac{\dot{\bar\rho}_\xi}{\bar\rho_\xi}
	+
	\sum_\alpha
	\frac{\tau_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi}
	\dot\phi_\xi
	+
	\sum_\alpha
	\frac{\tau_\xi^\alpha}{\eta_\xi^\alpha}
	\dot\eta_\xi^\alpha
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & -
	\sum_\alpha
	L_\xi^\alpha\dot c_\xi^\alpha
	-
	\nabla_{\mbf x}\cdot\mbf q_\xi
	+
	\rho_\xi r_\xi
	+
	\rho_\xi e_\xi^{\rm int},
	\label{eq:MC_energy_balance}
\end{align}
%
where
\(\widehat e_\xi^\alpha
=\widehat\psi_\xi^\alpha+\theta\widehat{\mathfrak{s}}_\xi^\alpha\).
The solid stress power is decomposed by
\(\mbf F=\mbf A_s\bar{\mbf F}_s\):
%
\begin{equation}
	\mbf L_s
	=
	\dot{\mbf A}_s\mbf A_s^{-1}
	+
	\mbf A_s
	\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^{-1}
	\mbf A_s^{-1}.
	\label{eq:MC_F_split_for_power}
\end{equation}
%
Using \eqref{eq:MC_solid_kinematic_splits}, the solid stress power is
%
\begin{align}
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}\bs\sigma_s':\mbf L_s
	={} &
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\dot{\mbf A}_s^e
		(\mbf A_s^e)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\mbf A_s^e)^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s^e
		\dot{\mbf A}_s^p
		(\mbf A_s^p)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\mbf A_s^p)^{-1}
		(\mbf A_s^e)^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s^e\mbf A_s^p
		\left.
		\p{\mbf A_s^\theta}{\theta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		(\mbf A_s^\theta)^{-1}
	\right]\dot\theta
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\sum_\beta
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\mbf A_s^p)^{-1}
		(\mbf A_s^e)^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s^e\mbf A_s^p
		\left.
		\p{\mbf A_s^\theta}{\eta_s^\beta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		(\mbf A_s^\theta)^{-1}
	\right]\dot\eta_s^\beta
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		\mbf A_s^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s
		\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^e
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
		\mbf A_s^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
		\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^p
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p)^{-1}
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
		\mbf A_s^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^p
		\left.
		\p{\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta}{\theta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta)^{-1}
	\right]\dot\theta
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\sum_\beta
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p)^{-1}
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
		\mbf A_s^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^p
		\left.
		\p{\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta}{\eta_s^\beta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta)^{-1}
	\right]\dot\eta_s^\beta .
	\label{eq:MC_stress_power_split}
\end{align}
%

The entropy inequality is
%
\begin{align}
	0\le{} &
	\sum_\xi
	\left[
	\rho_\xi\dot{\mathfrak{s}}_\xi
	+
	\sum_\alpha
	\dot c_\xi^\alpha
	\widehat{\mathfrak{s}}_\xi^\alpha
	+
	\nabla_{\mbf x}\cdot
	\left(
	\frac{\mbf q_\xi}{\theta}
	\right)
	-
	\frac{\rho_\xi r_\xi}{\theta}
	\right].
	\label{eq:MC_entropy_inequality}
\end{align}
%
Eliminating \(\rho_\xi r_\xi\) with \eqref{eq:MC_energy_balance}, adding the
identically vanishing mass-fraction tangent terms
\(\pi_\xi\sum_\alpha\dot\eta_\xi^\alpha\), and adding
\(\lambda\sum_\xi\dot\phi_\xi\), gives
%
\begin{align}
	0\le{} &
	\sum_\xi
	\begin{aligned}[t]
		 & \frac{1}{\theta}
		\left(
		\bs\sigma_\xi':\mbf L_\xi
		+
		\phi_\xi\bar p_\xi
		\frac{\dot{\bar\rho}_\xi}{\bar\rho_\xi}
		+
		\sum_\alpha
		\frac{\tau_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi}
		\dot\phi_\xi
		+
		\sum_\alpha
		\frac{\tau_\xi^\alpha}{\eta_\xi^\alpha}
		\dot\eta_\xi^\alpha
		\right.                                             \\
		 & \left.
		+
		\pi_\xi\sum_\alpha\dot\eta_\xi^\alpha
		-
		\rho_\xi
		\left(
		\dot\psi_\xi+\mathfrak{s}_\xi\dot\theta
		\right)
		-
		\sum_\alpha
		\dot c_\xi^\alpha
		\left(
		\widehat\psi_\xi^\alpha+L_\xi^\alpha
		\right)
		+
		\rho_\xi e_\xi^{\rm int}
		\right)                                             \\
		 & -
		\frac{\mbf q_\xi\cdot\nabla_{\mbf x}\theta}{\theta^2}
	\end{aligned}
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\frac{1}{\theta}
	\left(
	\lambda\sum_\xi\dot\phi_\xi
	-
	\sum_{f\in\mc F}\gamma_f\dot\phi_f
	-
	\phi
	\left.
	\p{\gamma}{\theta}
	\right\vert_{\{S_f\}_{f\in\mc F}}
	\dot\theta
	+
	\phi
	\left.
	\p{\gamma}{\theta}
	\right\vert_{\{S_f\}_{f\in\mc F}}
	\dot\theta
	\right).
	\label{eq:MC_reduced_entropy_inequality}
\end{align}
%

Substituting \eqref{eq:MC_fluid_free_energy_rate},
\eqref{eq:MC_solid_free_energy_rate}, \eqref{eq:MC_solid_density_rate}, and
\eqref{eq:MC_stress_power_split} gives the collected inequality:
%
\begin{align}
	0\le{} &
	\frac{1}{\theta}
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		\left(
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		-
		\rho_s
		\mbf A_s^e
		\left(
		\left.
		\p{\psi_s}{\mbf A_s^e}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		\right)^T
		\right)
		\dot{\mbf A}_s^e
		(\mbf A_s^e)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		\left(
		\mbf A_s^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s
		-
		\rho_s
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
		\left(
		\left.
		\p{\psi_s}{\bar{\mbf F}_s^e}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		\right)^T
		\right.
		\right.
		\notag                                                    \\
	 & \left.
		\left.
		+
		\left(
		\rho_s
		\left.
		\p{\psi_s}{\bar\rho_s}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		\bar\rho_s
		-
		\phi_s\bar p_s
		\right)\mbf I
		\right)
		\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^e
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\mbf A_s^e)^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s^e
		\dot{\mbf A}_s^p
		(\mbf A_s^p)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
		\mbf A_s^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
		\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^p
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{f\in\mc F}
	\left[
		\frac{\phi_f\bar p_f}{\bar\rho_f}
		-
		\rho_f
		\left.
		\p{\psi_f}{\bar\rho_f}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_f}
	\right]
	\dot{\bar\rho}_f
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{f\in\mc F}
	\sum_\beta
	\left[
		\frac{\tau_f^\beta}{\eta_f^\beta}
		+\pi_f
		-
		\rho_f
		\left.
		\p{\psi_f}{\eta_f^\beta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_f}
	\right]\dot\eta_f^\beta
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & +
	\sum_{s\in\mc S}
	\sum_\beta
	\left[
		\frac{\tau_s^\beta}{\eta_s^\beta}
		+\pi_s
		-
		\rho_s
		\left.
		\p{\psi_s}{\eta_s^\beta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}

		\right.

	\label{eq:MC_collected_entropy}
\end{align}

\subsection{Reversible restrictions}
\label{sec:MC_reversible_restrictions}

The coefficient of
\(\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^e(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}\) gives
%
\begin{align}
	\mbf A_s^{-1}
	(\bs\sigma_s')^T
	\mbf A_s
	={} &
	\rho_s
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
	\left(
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\bar{\mbf F}_s^e}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	\right)^T
	-
	\left(
	\rho_s
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\bar\rho_s}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	\bar\rho_s
	-
	\phi_s\bar p_s
	\right)\mbf I .
	\label{eq:MC_stress_before_pressure}
\end{align}
%
The density coefficients identify the phase pressures:
%
\begin{subequations}
	\label{eq:MC_phase_pressure_definitions}
	\begin{align}
		\bar p_f
		 & =
		\bar\rho_f^2
		\left.
		\p{\psi_f}{\bar\rho_f}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_f},
		\qquad
		f\in\mc F,
		\label{eq:MC_fluid_pressure_definition} \\
		\bar p_s
		 & =
		\bar\rho_s^2
		\left.
		\p{\psi_s}{\bar\rho_s}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}},
		\qquad
		s\in\mc S .
		\label{eq:MC_solid_pressure_definition}
	\end{align}
\end{subequations}
%
Substitution of \eqref{eq:MC_solid_pressure_definition} into
\eqref{eq:MC_stress_before_pressure} gives the single-prime solid stress:
%
\begin{equation}
	\bs\sigma_s'
	=
	\mbf A_s^{-T}
	\left(
	\rho_s
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\bar{\mbf F}_s^e}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^T
	\right)
	\mbf A_s^T .
	\label{eq:MC_solid_stress_definition}
\end{equation}
%
The reversible distension coefficient gives
%
\begin{equation}
	\bs\sigma_s'
	=
	\rho_s
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\mbf A_s^e}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	(\mbf A_s^e)^T .
	\label{eq:MC_distension_force_definition}
\end{equation}
%
The volume-fraction coefficients give
%
\begin{subequations}
	\label{eq:MC_volume_fraction_restrictions}
	\begin{align}
		0
		 & =
		\lambda
		+
		\sum_\alpha
		\frac{\tau_s^\alpha}{\phi_s},
		\qquad
		s\in\mc S,
		\label{eq:MC_solid_volume_fraction_restriction} \\
		0
		 & =
		\lambda
		+
		\sum_\alpha
		\frac{\tau_f^\alpha}{\phi_f}
		-\gamma_f,
		\qquad
		f\in\mc F .
		\label{eq:MC_fluid_volume_fraction_restriction}
	\end{align}
\end{subequations}
%
The fluid composition projection is
%
\begin{equation}
	\rho_f
	\left(
	\left.
	\p{\psi_f}{\eta_f^\beta}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_f}
	-
	\left.
	\p{\psi_f}{\eta_f^N}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_f}
	\right)
	=
	\frac{\tau_f^\beta}{\eta_f^\beta}
	-
	\frac{\tau_f^N}{\eta_f^N},
	\qquad
	\beta=1,\ldots,N-1 .
	\label{eq:MC_fluid_composition_projection}
\end{equation}
%
The multicomponent solid composition projection is
%
\begin{align}
	 & \rho_s
	\left(
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\eta_s^\beta}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	-
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\eta_s^N}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	\right)
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & \quad
	-
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\mbf A_s^p)^{-1}
		(\mbf A_s^e)^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s^e\mbf A_s^p
		\left(
		\left.
		\p{\mbf A_s^\theta}{\eta_s^\beta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		-
		\left.
		\p{\mbf A_s^\theta}{\eta_s^N}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		\right)
		(\mbf A_s^\theta)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & \quad
	-
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		\left(
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p)^{-1}
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
		\mbf A_s^{-1}
		(\bs\sigma_s')^T
		\mbf A_s
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^p
		\right)
		\right.
		\notag                                                    \\
	 & \left.
		\quad
		\left(
		\left.
		\p{\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta}{\eta_s^\beta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		-
		\left.
		\p{\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta}{\eta_s^N}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		\right)
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta)^{-1}
	\right]
	\notag                                                        \\
	 & \qquad
	=
	\frac{\tau_s^\beta}{\eta_s^\beta}
	-
	\frac{\tau_s^N}{\eta_s^N},
	\qquad
	\beta=1,\ldots,N-1 .
	\label{eq:MC_solid_composition_projection}
\end{align}

The entropy coefficient gives
%
\begin{align}
	\mathfrak{s}_f
	={} &
	-
	\left.
	\p{\psi_f}{\theta}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_f},
	\qquad
	f\in\mc F,
	\notag                                                        \\
	\rho_s\mathfrak{s}_s
	={} &
	-
	\rho_s
	\left.
	\p{\psi_s}{\theta}
	\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
	+
	\operatorname{tr}\left[
		(\mbf A_s^p)^{-1}

		\mbf A_s
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
		\bar{\mbf F}_s^p
		\left.
		\p{\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta}{\theta}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}

\end{align}

\subsection{Sources, residual dissipation, and closures}
\label{sec:MC_residual_dissipation}

Using \(\dot c_\xi^\alpha=\sum_m\nu_{\xi (m)}^\alpha\dot r_{(m)}\), the source
term in \eqref{eq:MC_collected_entropy} becomes
%
\begin{align}
	&-\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha
	\dot c_\xi^\alpha
	\left(
	\widehat\psi_\xi^\alpha+L_\xi^\alpha
	\right)
	\notag \\
	&\qquad
	=
	-\sum_m
	\left[
	\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha
	\nu_{\xi (m)}^\alpha
	\left(
	\widehat\psi_\xi^\alpha+L_\xi^\alpha
	\right)
	\right]\dot r_{(m)} .
	\label{eq:MC_helmholtz_source_projection}
\end{align}
%
Choose the conversion-work potential
%
\begin{equation}
	L_\xi^\alpha
	=
	\mu_\xi^\alpha-\widehat\psi_\xi^\alpha .
	\label{eq:MC_L_mu_psi_closure}
\end{equation}
%
Then the source projection becomes
%
\begin{equation}
	-\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha
	\dot c_\xi^\alpha\mu_\xi^\alpha
	=
	-\sum_m
	\left(
	\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha
	\nu_{\xi (m)}^\alpha\mu_\xi^\alpha
	\right)\dot r_{(m)} ,
	\label{eq:MC_mu_source_projection}
\end{equation}
%
and the mechanism affinity is
%
\begin{equation}
	\mc A_{(m)}
	=
	-
	\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha
	\mu_\xi^\alpha\nu_{\xi (m)}^\alpha .
	\label{eq:MC_affinity_projection}
\end{equation}
%
The same admissible \(L_\xi^\alpha\) gives the component conversion relation
%
\begin{equation}
	\frac{D_\xi\mu_\xi^\alpha}{Dt}
	=
	\frac{1}{2}
	\mbf v_\xi\cdot\mbf v_\xi
	-
	\mu_\xi^\alpha
	-
	\frac{\tau_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi\eta_\xi^\alpha}.
	\label{eq:MC_admissible_conversion_component}
\end{equation}
%

After the reversible coefficients are set to zero, the residual inequality is
%
\begin{equation}
	\begin{aligned}
		0\le{}\; &
		\sum_m\mc A_{(m)}\dot r_{(m)}
		+
		\sum_{s\in\mc S}
		\bs\sigma_s':
		\mbf A_s^e
		\dot{\mbf A}_s^p
		(\mbf A_s^p)^{-1}
		(\mbf A_s^e)^{-1}
		\\
		&+
		\sum_{s\in\mc S}
		\operatorname{tr}\left[
			(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^{-1}
			\mbf A_s^{-1}
			\left(
			(\bs\sigma_s')^T
			-
			\frac{1}{3}
			\operatorname{tr}(\bs\sigma_s')\mbf I
			\right)
			\mbf A_s
			\bar{\mbf F}_s^e
			\dot{\bar{\mbf F}}_s^p
			(\bar{\mbf F}_s^p)^{-1}
		\right]
		\\
		&+
		\sum_\xi\rho_\xi e_\xi^{\rm int}
		-
		\sum_\xi
		\frac{\mbf q_\xi\cdot\nabla_{\mbf x}\theta}{\theta}.
	\end{aligned}
	\label{eq:MC_residual_dissipation}
\end{equation}
%
Reaction kinetics must make
\(\sum_m\mc A_{(m)}\dot r_{(m)}\ge0\).  Plastic flow laws must make the two
solid plastic powers nonnegative.  Interphase exchange and heat conduction may
be closed by
%
\begin{subequations}
	\label{eq:MC_interaction_heat_closures}
	\begin{align}
		\mbf b_{\zeta\xi}
		 & =
		-\mbf k_{\xi\zeta}^{-1}
		(\mbf v_\xi-\mbf v_\zeta),
		\qquad
		\mbf k_{\xi\zeta}=\mbf k_{\xi\zeta}^T>0,
		\label{eq:MC_interphase_exchange_law} \\
		\mbf q_\xi
		 & =
		-\mbf K_\xi\nabla_{\mbf x}\theta,
		\qquad
		\mbf K_\xi=\mbf K_\xi^T\ge0 .
		\label{eq:MC_heat_flux_law}
	\end{align}
\end{subequations}
%
Together with pairwise momentum exchange and total interaction-energy
conservation, these choices make the interaction and heat-conduction
contributions nonnegative.

\subsection{Additive component specialization}
\label{sec:MC_additive_component_specialization}

The generic derivation above does not assume that the solid Helmholtz energy is
additive.  The additive model is obtained by choosing component potentials
\(\psi_s^\alpha\) and writing
%
\begin{equation}
	\psi_s
	=
	\sum_\alpha
	\eta_s^\alpha
	\psi_s^\alpha(
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^e,\mbf A_s^e,
	\bar\rho_s^\alpha,\eta_s^\beta,\theta),
	\qquad
	\widehat\psi_s^\alpha=\psi_s^\alpha .
	\label{eq:MC_additive_free_energy}
\end{equation}
%
Then the pressure, stress, and distension restrictions become
%
\begin{subequations}
	\label{eq:MC_additive_mixture_rules}
	\begin{align}
		\bar p_s
		 & =
		\sum_\alpha
		\phi_s^\alpha\bar p_s^\alpha,
		\label{eq:MC_additive_pressure_average} \\
		\bar p_s^\alpha
		 & =
		(\bar\rho_s^\alpha)^2
		\left.
		\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{\bar\rho_s^\alpha}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}},
		\label{eq:MC_additive_component_pressure} \\
		\bs\sigma_s'
		 & =
		\sum_\alpha
		\bs\sigma_s^{\prime\alpha},
		\label{eq:MC_additive_stress_sum} \\
		\bs\sigma_s^{\prime\alpha}
		 & =
		\rho_s^\alpha
		\mbf A_s^{-T}
		\left(
		\left.
		\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{\bar{\mbf F}_s^e}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		(\bar{\mbf F}_s^e)^T
		\right)
		\mbf A_s^T,
		\label{eq:MC_additive_component_stress} \\
		\bs\sigma_s'
		 & =
		\rho_s
		\sum_\alpha
		\eta_s^\alpha
		\left.
		\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{\mbf A_s^e}
		\right\vert_{\mc X_s^{\rm mc}}
		(\mbf A_s^e)^T .
		\label{eq:MC_additive_distension_force}
	\end{align}
\end{subequations}
%
The fixed-pressure transformation may be performed componentwise.  Define
\(\bar v_s^\alpha=1/\bar\rho_s^\alpha\) and
\(v_{s0}^\alpha=1/\rho_{s0}^\alpha\).  The component nonlinear Biot coefficient
is
%
\begin{equation}
	B_s^\alpha
	\equiv
	1
	-
	\frac{a_s}{v_{s0}^\alpha}
	\left.
	\p{\bar v_s^\alpha}{J}
	\right\vert_{\bar p_s^\alpha,\theta,\eta_s^\beta}.
	\label{eq:MC_additive_component_biot}
\end{equation}
%
The component stress split is
%
\begin{equation}
	\bs\sigma_s^{\prime\alpha}
	=
	\bs\sigma_s^{\prime\prime\alpha}
	+
	(1-B_s^\alpha)\bar p_s^\alpha\mbf I .
	\label{eq:MC_additive_component_biot_split}
\end{equation}
%
Summing components gives the internal solid mixture rule
%
\begin{subequations}
	\label{eq:MC_additive_biot_mixture_rule}
	\begin{align}
		\bs\sigma_s^{\prime\prime}
		 & =
		\sum_\alpha
		\bs\sigma_s^{\prime\prime\alpha},
		\label{eq:MC_additive_double_prime_sum} \\
		\bs\sigma_s'
		 & =
		\bs\sigma_s^{\prime\prime}
		+
		(1-B_s)\bar p_s\mbf I,
		\label{eq:MC_additive_phase_biot_split} \\
		B_s
		 & =
		1
		-
		\frac{1}{\bar p_s}
		\sum_\alpha
		(1-B_s^\alpha)\bar p_s^\alpha .
		\label{eq:MC_additive_biot_mixture_coefficient}
	\end{align}
\end{subequations}
%
This additive specialization is the old multicomponent-solid mixture-rule
structure, now embedded in the full entropy inequality with plastic,
distension, thermal, and composition-dependent stress-free terms retained.

\subsection{Examples and single-component specialization}
\label{sec:MC_examples_single_component_specialization}

An absorption/desorption mechanism transferring component \(g\) between a
mobile phase \(f\) and a solid phase \(s\) may be written
%
\begin{equation}
	\nu_{s (m)}^g=1,
	\qquad
	\nu_{f (m)}^g=-1,
	\qquad
	\nu_{\xi (m)}^\alpha=0
	\quad\hbox{otherwise}.
	\label{eq:MC_sorption_stoichiometry}
\end{equation}
%
The affinity is
%
\begin{equation}
	\mc A_{(m)}
	=
	\mu_f^g-\mu_s^g .
	\label{eq:MC_sorption_affinity}
\end{equation}
%
An admissible kinetic law must satisfy
\(\mc A_{(m)}\dot r_{(m)}\ge0\).  The absorbed amount \(\eta_s^g\) can also
enter \(\psi_s\), \(\mbf A_s^\theta\), \(\bar{\mbf F}_s^\theta\), and the
plastic flow law, so sorption can change stiffness, yield strength, swelling,
and compaction behavior in one thermodynamic state.

For methane hydrate or a clathrate solid, host water and guest methane are solid
components of the same load-bearing solid phase.  Hydrate formation and
dissociation are conversion mechanisms between mobile components and solid
components.  Cage occupancy changes the solid composition; that composition may
change the free energy, thermal expansion, stress-free cage volume, and plastic
response without introducing a second solid displacement field.

The single-component mineral solid used in the following section is recovered by
choosing one active component and imposing
%
\begin{equation}
	\eta_s^s=1,
	\qquad
	\dot\eta_s^s=0,
	\qquad
	\phi_s^s=1,
	\qquad
	\bar\rho_s^s=\bar\rho_s .
	\label{eq:MC_single_component_constraints}
\end{equation}
%
Then \eqref{eq:MC_solid_general_free_energy} reduces to
%
\begin{equation}
	\psi_s
	=
	\psi_s(
	\bar{\mbf F}_s^e,\mbf A_s^e,\bar\rho_s,\theta),
	\label{eq:MC_single_component_free_energy}
\end{equation}
%
the solid composition projection \eqref{eq:MC_solid_composition_projection}
vanishes identically, and the additive mixture rules reduce to
%
\begin{equation}
	\bar p_s^s=\bar p_s,
	\qquad
	\bs\sigma_s^{\prime s}=\bs\sigma_s',
	\qquad
	B_s^s=B_s .
	\label{eq:MC_single_component_reductions}
\end{equation}
%
Thus the current one-component solid entropy inequality is the specialization
obtained by imposing \(\eta_s^s=1\) before the Coleman--Noll coefficients are
collected.
