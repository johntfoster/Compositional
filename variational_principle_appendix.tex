\section{Variational Derivation}
\label{app:variational}
\label{sec:variational}

We derive the governing equations from Hamilton's extended principle, following Bedford \& Drumheller~\cite{drumheller1980thermomechanical} and Drumheller~\cite{drumheller2000}, adapted to a partially saturated four-phase system (solid, aquous, oelic, gas) and arbitrary miscible components $\alpha$ within each phase and with porosity-dependent solid free energies.

Each component has volume fraction~$\phi_\xi$, intrinsic density~$\bar\rho_\xi$, bulk density $\rho_\xi = \phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi$, velocity~$\mbf{v}_\xi$, deformation gradient~$\mbf{F}_\xi$, and Jacobian $J_\xi = \det(\mbf{F}_\xi)$.  The solid phases share a common trajectory ($\mbf{v}_A = \mbf{v}_B = \mbf{v}_s$, $\mbf{F}_A = \mbf{F}_B = \mbf{F}_s$, $J_A = J_B = J_s$).  The total porosity is $\phi = \phi_f + \phi_g$, the fluid saturation is $S_f = \phi_f/\phi$, and the mass added per unit reference volume to component~$\xi$ by reactions is~$c_\xi$, with rate per unit current volume $c^+_\xi = \dot{c}_\xi / J_\xi$.

The free energies of the solid phases are written as $\psi_A(\mbf{F}_s, \phi)$ and $\psi_B(\mbf{F}_s, \phi)$, retaining the full deformation gradient and the current porosity as independent arguments.  The multiplicative decomposition $\mbf{F}_s = \mbf{A}_s \bar{\mbf F}_s$ is \emph{not} imposed at this stage; it enters later in the Coleman--Noll procedure (Appendix~\ref{sec:coleman}) when a specific constitutive form is adopted.

\subsection{Variational principle and its terms}

The extended Hamilton's principle is
\begin{equation*}
  \int_{t_1}^{t_2}\left[\delta(T - U) + \delta W + \sum_k \delta C_k\right] dt = 0,
\end{equation*}
with kinetic energy, internal energy, virtual work, and four constraints:
\begin{align}
  T &= \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}} \tfrac{1}{2}\rho_\xi\,\mbf{v}_\xi\cdot\mbf{v}_\xi \,\dv,
  \label{eq:T_def} \\
  U &= \sum_\alpha \left[\int_{\mc{B}_{s 0}} \rho^\alpha_{s0}\,\psi^\alpha_s(\mbf{F}_s, \bar\rho^\alpha_s, \eta^\alpha_s,  \theta^\alpha_s, \phi) \eta^\alpha_s \,  dV_s \right. \notag \\
    &\qquad + \int_{\mc{B}} \rho^\alpha_{a}\,\psi_a^\alpha(\bar\rho_a^\beta, \eta_a^\beta, \theta^\beta_a)  \, dv \notag \\ 
    &\qquad + \int_{\mc{B}} \rho^\alpha_{o}\,\psi^\alpha_o(\bar\rho_o^\beta, \eta_o^\beta, \theta^\beta_o)  \,dv \\
    &\qquad + \int_{\mc{B}} \rho^\alpha_{g}\,\psi^\alpha_g(\bar\rho^\alpha_g, \eta_g^\beta, \theta^\beta_g)  \,dv \notag \\
    &\left.\qquad + \int_{\mc{B}} \gamma_{oa}(S_a) \,\dv + \int_{\mc{B}} \gamma_{go}(S_g)\,\dv \right], 
  \label{eq:U_def} \\
  \delta W &= \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}} \left[(\rho_\xi\mbf{g} + \mbf{b}_\xi)\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi + \sum_\alpha \mc{L}^\alpha_\xi\,\frac{\delta c^\alpha_\xi}{J_\xi}\right] \,\dv,
  \label{eq:W_def} \\
  C_1 &= \int_{\mc{B}} \lambda\left(\sum_\xi\phi_\xi - 1\right) \,\dv,
  \label{eq:C1_def} \\
  C_2 &= \sum_\xi \sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi 0}} \mu_\xi^\alpha \left( J_\xi \eta_\xi^\alpha - \frac{\phi_{\xi 0} \bar\rho_{\xi 0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi \bar\rho_\xi^\alpha} \right) \eta^\alpha_\xi dV_\xi,
  \label{eq:C2_def} \\
  C_3 &= \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi 0}} \pi_\xi \left( \sum_\alpha \eta_\xi^\alpha - 1 \right) dV_\xi,
  \label{eq:C3_def} \\
  C_4 &= \int_{\mc{B}} \tau\sum_\alpha\sum_\xi \frac{\dot{c}^\alpha_\xi}{J_\xi} \,\dv.
  \label{eq:C4_def}
\end{align}
The Helmholtz free energies are $\psi^\alpha_s$, $\psi_a^\alpha$, $\psi_o^\alpha$, and $\psi_g^\alpha$.  The interaction forces satisfy $\sum_\xi \mbf{b}_\xi = \mbf{0}$, and $\mc{L}^\alpha_\xi$ is a generalized force conjugate to mass transfer (identified later via Coleman--Noll).  The Lagrange multipliers~$\lambda$, $\mu^\alpha_\xi$, $\pi_\xi$, and~$\tau$ enforce the volume fraction constraint, component mass conservation, component partitioning, and mixture mass balance, respectively.

We treat $\mbf{x}_\xi$, $\eta^\alpha_\xi$, $\phi_\xi$, $\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi$, $\theta_\xi^\alpha$, and $c_\xi^\alpha$ as independent fields and compute the variations in what follows.

% ============================================================
\subsection{Variation of the kinetic energy}
\label{sec:var_T_app}
% ============================================================

The kinetic energy depends on the phase velocities and bulk densities. Treating $\rho_\xi = \phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi$ as determined by $\phi_\xi$ and $\bar\rho_\xi$ (varied separately), the variation at fixed densities is
\begin{equation}
  \delta T = \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}} \rho_\xi\,\mbf{v}_\xi \cdot \delta\mbf{v}_\xi \,\dv.
\end{equation}
Since $\delta\mbf{v}_\xi = \mathrm{d}(\delta\mbf{x}_\xi)/\mathrm{d}t$ and $\delta\mbf{x}_\xi\vert_{t_1} = \delta\mbf{x}_\xi\vert_{t_2} = \mbf{0}$, integration by parts in time gives
\begin{equation}
  \int_{t_1}^{t_2} \delta T\,\mathrm{d}t
  = -\int_{t_1}^{t_2} \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}} \rho_\xi\,\mbf{a}_\xi \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_\xi \,\dv\,\mathrm{d}t.
  \label{eq:deltaT_app}
\end{equation}

% ============================================================
\subsection{Variation of the solid internal energy w.r.t.\ $\mbf{x}_s$}
\label{sec:var_Us_F}
% ============================================================

The solid contribution is
\begin{align}
  \delta U_s = \int_{\mc{B}_{s0}} \sum_\alpha &\rho^\alpha_{s 0} \eta_s^\alpha \left[\left.\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{F_{kl}}\right\vert_{\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\eta^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s,\phi} \delta F_{kl} \right. \notag \\
  &\qquad + \left.\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{\bar\rho^\alpha_s}\right\vert_{\mbf{F},\eta^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s,\phi} \delta \bar\rho^\alpha_s \notag \\
  &\qquad + \left.\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{\eta^\alpha_s}\right\vert_{\mbf{F},\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s,\phi} \delta \eta^\alpha_s \notag \\
  &\qquad + \left.\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{\theta^\alpha_s}\right\vert_{\mbf{F},\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\eta^\alpha_s,\phi} \delta \theta^\alpha_s \notag \\
  &\qquad + \left.\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{\phi}\right\vert_{\mbf{F},\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\eta^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s} \left.\delta\phi\right|_{\mbf{X}_s} \notag \\.
  &\qquad \left. + \frac{\psi^\alpha_s}{\eta_s^\alpha} \delta \eta^\alpha_s \right] dV_s
\end{align}
%
The variation $\delta F_{kl} = \partial(\delta x_{s,k})/\partial X_{s,l}$ produces the standard stress divergence upon integration by parts.  Transforming to the current configuration gives
\begin{equation}
  \int_{\mc{B}_{s0}} \rho_{s 0}\,\sum_\alpha \left.\p{\psi_s^\alpha}{F_{kl}}\right\vert_{\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\eta^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s,\phi} \delta F_{kl}\, dV_s = -\int_{\mc{B}} \nabla_{\mbf{x}} \cdot \bs\sigma'_\xi \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_s \,\dv + \text{boundary terms},
  \label{eq:stress_variation}
\end{equation}
where the Terzaghi effective Cauchy stress\footnote{i.e.\ density held fixed} is defined through
\begin{align}
  \bs\sigma'_s &\equiv  \frac{1}{J_s} \,\sum_\alpha \rho^\alpha_{s 0}\eta^\alpha_s \left.\p{\psi^\alpha_s}{\mbf{F}}\right\vert_{\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\eta^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s,\phi}\, \mbf{F}^T \\
  &\equiv \frac{1}{J_s} \rho_{s 0}\,\left.\p{\psi_s}{\mbf{F}}\right\vert_{\bar\rho_s,\eta_s,\theta_s,\phi}\, \mbf{F}^T .
  \label{eq:var_cauchy_def}
\end{align}
The notation $\vert_{\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\eta^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s,\phi}$ emphasizes that this derivative is taken holding all other free-energy arguments fixed.

% ============================================================
\subsection{Variation of the solid energy w.r.t.\ porosity via the inter-component identity}
\label{sec:var_Us_phi}
% ============================================================

The porosity~$\phi = \phi_a + \phi_o + \phi_g$ is a spatial field naturally associated with the combination of the aqueas, oliec, and gas phases, yet it appears as an argument of the solid free energies $\psi^\alpha_s(\mbf{F}_s, \bar\rho^\alpha_s, \eta^\alpha_s, \theta^\alpha_s, \phi)$ in the integral over the solid reference configuration.  Since this integral is taken at fixed~$\mbf{X}_s$, we require the variation of~$\phi$ at fixed~$\mbf{X}_s$.  Using the inter-component variation identity (equation~(1.26) of Bedford \& Drumheller~\cite{bedford1983theories})
\begin{equation}
  \left.\delta\Gamma_{(\zeta)}\right|_{\mbf{X}_{(\xi)}} = \delta\Gamma_{(\zeta)} - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\Gamma_{(\zeta)} \cdot \left(\delta\mbf{x}_{(\zeta)} - \delta\mbf{x}_{(\xi)}\right),
  \label{eq:BD_identity}
\end{equation}
we obtain
\begin{align}
  \left.\delta\phi\right|_{\mbf{X}_s}
  &= \delta\phi_a + \delta\phi_o + \delta\phi_g - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_a \cdot \left(\delta\mbf{x}_a - \delta\mbf{x}_s\right)- \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_o \cdot \left(\delta\mbf{x}_o - \delta\mbf{x}_s\right) - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g \cdot \left(\delta\mbf{x}_g - \delta\mbf{x}_s\right) \notag \\
  &= \delta\phi_a + \delta\phi_o + \delta\phi_g - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_a \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_a -\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_o \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_o -  \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_g + \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_s.
  \label{eq:delta_phi}
\end{align}
Defining
\begin{align}
  P^\star_\phi &\equiv \frac{1}{J_s} \sum_\alpha \rho^\alpha_{s 0} \eta_s^\alpha \left.\p{\psi^\alpha_s}{\phi}\right\vert_{\mbf{F}_s,\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\eta^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s} \notag \\ 
  &\equiv \frac{1}{J_s} \rho_{s 0}\left.\p{\psi_s}{\phi}\right\vert_{\mbf{F}_s,\bar\rho_s,\eta_s,\theta_s},
  \label{eq:Pstar_phi}
\end{align}
and substituting~\eqref{eq:delta_phi} into~\eqref{eq:deltaU_solid}, the porosity-dependent part of the solid energy variation becomes:
\begin{align}
  \int_{\mc{B}_{s0}} \sum_\alpha \rho^\alpha_{s 0} \eta^\alpha_s \,\left.\p{\psi^\alpha_s}{\phi}\right\vert_{\mbf{F}_s,\bar\rho^\alpha_s,\eta^\alpha_s,\theta^\alpha_s}\, \left.\delta\phi\right|_{\mbf{X}_s}\, dV_s
  &= \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \delta\phi_a \,\dv + P^\star_\phi\, \delta\phi_o \,\dv +  \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \delta\phi_g \,\dv \notag \\
  &\quad - \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_a \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_a \,\dv - \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_o \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_o \,\dv \notag \\
  &\quad  - \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_g + \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_s \,\dv.
  \label{eq:phi_variation_split}
\end{align}


% ============================================================
\subsection{Variation of the interfacial energies}
\label{sec:var_gamma}
% ============================================================

With three mobile phases (aqueous, oleic, and gas), let
\[
  \phi = \phi_a + \phi_o + \phi_g,
  \qquad
  S_a = \frac{\phi_a}{\phi},
  \qquad
  S_g = \frac{\phi_g}{\phi},
\]
so that $S_o = 1-S_a-S_g = \phi_o/\phi$.  We take the interfacial energy to be
\[
  U_\gamma = \int_{\mc{B}} \gamma_{oa}(S_a)\,\dv + \int_{\mc{B}} \gamma_{go}(S_g)\,\dv,
\]
which introduces two independent capillary pressures,
\begin{equation}
  p_{c,oa} \equiv -\frac{1}{\phi}\frac{\partial\gamma_{oa}}{\partial S_a} > 0,
  \qquad
  p_{c,og} \equiv -\frac{1}{\phi}\frac{\partial\gamma_{go}}{\partial S_g} > 0.
  \label{eq:pc_def}
\end{equation}
Using
\[
  \delta S_a = \frac{\phi_o+\phi_g}{\phi^2}\,\delta\phi_a - \frac{\phi_a}{\phi^2}(\delta\phi_o+\delta\phi_g),
  \qquad
  \delta S_g = \frac{\phi_a+\phi_o}{\phi^2}\,\delta\phi_g - \frac{\phi_g}{\phi^2}(\delta\phi_a+\delta\phi_o),
\]
together with the inter-component variation identity for the spatial fields, the variation of the interfacial energy contributes
\begin{align}
  \delta U_\gamma
  &= \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
      \left(-p_{c,oa}\frac{\phi_o+\phi_g}{\phi} + p_{c,og}\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\right)\delta\phi_a \notag \\
  &\qquad\qquad
      + \left(p_{c,oa}\frac{\phi_a}{\phi} + p_{c,og}\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\right)\delta\phi_o \notag \\
  &\qquad\qquad
      + \left(p_{c,oa}\frac{\phi_a}{\phi} - p_{c,og}\frac{\phi_a+\phi_o}{\phi}\right)\delta\phi_g
    \Bigg] \,\dv \notag \\
  &\quad + \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
      \left(-p_{c,oa}\frac{\phi_o+\phi_g}{\phi} + p_{c,og}\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\right)\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_a \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_a \notag \\
  &\qquad\qquad
      + \left(p_{c,oa}\frac{\phi_a}{\phi} + p_{c,og}\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\right)\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_o \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_o \notag \\
  &\qquad\qquad
      + \left(p_{c,oa}\frac{\phi_a}{\phi} - p_{c,og}\frac{\phi_a+\phi_o}{\phi}\right)\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_g
    \Bigg] \,\dv.
  \label{eq:deltaU_fg}
\end{align}

% ============================================================
\subsection{Variation of fluid, oleic, and gas energy w.r.t.\ intrinsic density $\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi$}
\label{sec:var_Ufog_rho}
% ============================================================

Extending the identity (1.28) of Druheller and Beford to our system of multiphase multicomponent flow, it becomes

\begin{equation}
  \delta \int_{\mc{B}} \rho^\alpha_\xi \psi^\alpha_\xi \dv = \int_{\mc{B}} \rho^\alpha_\xi \delta \psi^\alpha_\xi + \psi^\alpha_\xi \frac{\delta c^\alpha_\xi}{\eta^\alpha_\xi J_\xi} \dv
  \label{eq:bd128}
\end{equation}

We now adopt the hybrid choice in which the free energies remain constitutive functions,
\(\psi^\alpha_\xi = \psi^\alpha_\xi(\bar\rho^\beta_\xi,\eta^\beta_\xi,\theta^\beta_\xi)\), but the intrinsic densities
\(\bar\rho^\beta_\xi\) are treated as independent kinematic variables for each component
\(\beta\) in the mobile phase \(\xi\in\{a,o,g\}\).  For component \(\alpha\), let
\(\rho^\alpha_\xi \equiv \phi_\xi\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi\).  Then
\begin{equation}
  U_\xi = \sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}} \rho^\alpha_\xi\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\dv.
  \label{eq:U_xi_current}
\end{equation}
Using the identity~\eqref{eq:bd128} directly in the current configuration gives, for each
component \(\alpha\),
\begin{equation}
  \left.\delta U_\xi\right\vert_\alpha
  = \int_{\mc{B}} \left(\rho^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\psi^\alpha_\xi
  + \psi^\alpha_\xi\,\frac{\delta c^\alpha_\xi}{\eta^\alpha_\xi J_\xi}\right)\dv.
  \label{eq:deltaUfog_rho_alpha_identity}
\end{equation}
Since
\begin{equation}
  \delta\psi^\alpha_\xi
  = \sum_\beta
  \left.\p{\psi^\alpha_\xi}{\bar\rho^\beta_\xi}\right\vert_{\eta^\kappa_\xi,\theta^\kappa_\xi,\bar\rho^{\kappa\neq\beta}_\xi}
  \delta\bar\rho^\beta_\xi,
  \label{eq:delta_psi_rho}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\delta c^\alpha_\xi}{\eta^\alpha_\xi J_\xi}
  = \delta\rho^\alpha_\xi
  = \phi_\xi\,\delta\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi + \bar\rho^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\phi_\xi,
  \label{eq:delta_c_rho_relation}
\end{equation}
we obtain
\begin{align}
  \left.\delta U_\xi\right\vert_\alpha
  &= \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
    \rho^\alpha_\xi
    \sum_\beta
    \left.\p{\psi^\alpha_\xi}{\bar\rho^\beta_\xi}\right\vert_{\eta^\kappa_\xi,\theta^\kappa_\xi,\bar\rho^{\kappa\neq\beta}_\xi}
    \delta\bar\rho^\beta_\xi
    + \psi^\alpha_\xi\left(\phi_\xi\,\delta\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi + \bar\rho^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\phi_\xi\right)
  \Bigg] \dv.
  \label{eq:deltaUfog_rho_alpha_expanded}
\end{align}
Summing over all components in the mobile phase gives
\begin{equation}
  \left.\delta U_\xi\right\vert_{\delta\bar\rho_\xi}
  = \sum_\alpha \left.\delta U_\xi\right\vert_\alpha,
  \label{eq:deltaUfog_rho_phase}
\end{equation}
and therefore the complete current-configuration virtual-work statement for the mobile phases is
\begin{align}
  \left.\delta(U_a+U_o+U_g)\right\vert_{\delta\bar\rho}
  &= \sum_{\xi\in\{a,o,g\}}\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
    \rho^\alpha_\xi
    \sum_\beta
    \left.\p{\psi^\alpha_\xi}{\bar\rho^\beta_\xi}\right\vert_{\eta^\kappa_\xi,\theta^\kappa_\xi,\bar\rho^{\kappa\neq\beta}_\xi}
    \delta\bar\rho^\beta_\xi 
    + \psi^\alpha_\xi\left(\phi_\xi\,\delta\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi + \bar\rho^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\phi_\xi\right)
  \Bigg] \dv.
  \label{eq:deltaUfog_rho_total}
\end{align}
Here and below, the notation
\(\left.\partial \psi^\alpha_\xi / \partial \bar\rho^\beta_\xi\right\vert_{\eta^\kappa_\xi,\theta^\kappa_\xi,\bar\rho^{\kappa\neq\beta}_\xi}\)
means that all \(\eta^\kappa_\xi\), all \(\theta^\kappa_\xi\), and all \(\bar\rho^\kappa_\xi\) with
\(\kappa \neq \beta\) are held fixed.  The coefficients of the independent current variations
\(\delta\bar\rho^\beta_\xi\) and \(\delta\phi_\xi\) are then read directly from
\eqref{eq:deltaUfog_rho_total}.  The effect of the motion through \(J_\xi\) is already absorbed into
identity~\eqref{eq:bd128}, so no separate convective term appears here.

\subsection{Variation of fluid, oleic, and gas energy w.r.t.\ partial volume $\eta^\alpha_\xi$}
\label{sec:var_Ufog_eta}
% ============================================================

Following the same pull-back, coefficient-collection, and push-forward steps used in
Section~\ref{sec:var_Ufog_rho}, but now with $\eta^\beta_\xi$ as the free-energy argument, the
complete current-configuration virtual-work statement is
\begin{align}
  \left.\delta(U_a + U_o + U_g)\right\vert_{\delta\eta}
  &= \sum_{\xi\in\{a,o,g\}} \sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
  \Bigg[
    \phi_\xi\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi
    + \bar\rho^\alpha_\xi\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\phi_\xi
    + \rho^\alpha_\xi\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\!\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi \notag \\
  &\qquad\qquad
    + \frac{\rho^\alpha_\xi}{\eta^\alpha_\xi}\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\eta^\alpha_\xi
    + \rho^\alpha_\xi
      \sum_\beta
      \left.\p{\psi^\alpha_\xi}{\eta^\beta_\xi}\right\vert_{\bar\rho^\kappa_\xi,\theta^\kappa_\xi,\eta^{\kappa\neq\beta}_\xi}
      \delta\eta^\beta_\xi
  \Bigg] \dv.
  \label{eq:deltaUfog_eta}
\end{align}

\subsection{Variation of fluid, oleic, and gas energy w.r.t.\ temperature $\theta^\alpha_\xi$}
\label{sec:var_Ufog_theta}
% ============================================================

Likewise, following the same operations as in Section~\ref{sec:var_Ufog_rho}, but now with
$\theta^\beta_\xi$ as the free-energy argument, we obtain the current-configuration statement
\begin{align}
  \left.\delta(U_a + U_o + U_g)\right\vert_{\delta\theta}
  &= \sum_{\xi\in\{a,o,g\}} \sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
  \Bigg[
    \phi_\xi\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi
    + \bar\rho^\alpha_\xi\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\phi_\xi
    + \rho^\alpha_\xi\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\!\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi \notag \\
  &\qquad\qquad
    + \frac{\rho^\alpha_\xi}{\eta^\alpha_\xi}\,\psi^\alpha_\xi\,\delta\eta^\alpha_\xi
    + \rho^\alpha_\xi
      \sum_\beta
      \left.\p{\psi^\alpha_\xi}{\theta^\beta_\xi}\right\vert_{\bar\rho^\kappa_\xi,\eta^\kappa_\xi,\theta^{\kappa\neq\beta}_\xi}
      \delta\theta^\beta_\xi
  \Bigg] \dv.
  \label{eq:deltaUfog_theta}
\end{align}

% ============================================================
\subsection{Variation of constraint $C_1$: volume-fraction saturation}
\label{sec:var_C1_app}
% ============================================================

The constraint $C_1 = \int_{\mc{B}} \lambda(\sum_\xi\phi_\xi - 1)\,\dv$ is varied with respect to $\lambda$ and the constituent motions. Using the Bedford--Drumheller rule for a current field attached to constituent $\xi$,
\begin{equation}
  \left.\delta\phi_\xi\right\vert_{\mbf{X}_\xi}
  = \delta\phi_\xi - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_\xi\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi,
  \label{eq:delta_phi_BD_local}
\end{equation}
we obtain
\begin{align}
  \delta C_1
  &= \int_{\mc{B}} \delta\lambda \left(\sum_\xi\phi_\xi - 1\right) \dv \,
  + \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}} \lambda\left(\left.\delta\phi_\xi\right\vert_{\mbf{X}_\xi}\right) \dv \notag \\
  &= \int_{\mc{B}} \delta\lambda \left(\sum_\xi\phi_\xi - 1\right) \dv
  + \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}} \lambda\left(\delta\phi_\xi - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_\xi\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi\right) \dv.
  \label{eq:deltaC1_app}
\end{align}
The first line enforces the saturation constraint $\sum_\xi\phi_\xi = 1$. The remaining terms contribute $\lambda$ to each $\delta\phi_\xi$ equation and $-\lambda\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_\xi$ to each momentum variation $\delta\mbf{x}_\xi$.

% ============================================================
\subsection{Variation of constraint $C_2$: component material mass balance}
\label{sec:var_C2_app}
% ============================================================

Let $h_\xi^\alpha \equiv J_\xi\eta_\xi^\alpha - (\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha)/(\phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha)$, so $C_2 = \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha\int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}\mu_\xi^\alpha h_\xi^\alpha\,dV_\xi$.  Computing $\delta h_\xi^\alpha$:
\begin{equation}
  \delta h_\xi^\alpha
  = \eta_\xi^\alpha\,\delta J_\xi
  + J_\xi\,\delta\eta_\xi^\alpha
  + \frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi^2\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}\,\delta\phi_\xi
  + \frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi(\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha)^2}\,\delta\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha
  - \frac{1}{\phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}\,\delta c_\xi^\alpha.
  \label{eq:dh}
\end{equation}
The full variation is
\begin{align}
  \delta C_2
  &= \underbrace{\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \delta\mu_\xi^\alpha\, h_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi}_{\text{enforces } h_\xi^\alpha = 0}
  \notag \\
  &\quad
  + \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \mu_\xi^\alpha\,\eta_\xi^\alpha\,\delta J_\xi \, dV_\xi
  \notag \\
  &\quad
  + \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \mu_\xi^\alpha\,J_\xi\,\delta\eta_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi
  \notag \\
  &\quad
  + \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \mu_\xi^\alpha\,\frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi^2\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}\,\delta\phi_\xi \, dV_\xi
  \notag \\
  &\quad
  + \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \mu_\xi^\alpha\,\frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi(\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha)^2}\,\delta\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi
  \notag \\
  &\quad
  - \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \frac{\mu_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}\,\delta c_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi.
  \label{eq:deltaC2_lines}
\end{align}

\paragraph{$\delta J_\xi$ terms (from $\delta\mbf{x}_\xi$):}
Using $\delta J_\xi = J_\xi\,\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi$ and pushing forward via $dv = J_\xi\,dV_\xi$:
\begin{align}
  \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
    \mu_\xi^\alpha\,\eta_\xi^\alpha\,J_\xi\,\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi \, dV_\xi
  &= \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
    \mu_\xi^\alpha\,\eta_\xi^\alpha\,\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi \, \dv
  \notag \\
  &= -\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
    \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\!\left(\mu_\xi^\alpha\,\eta_\xi^\alpha\right) \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_\xi \, \dv
    + \text{b.t.}
  \label{eq:C2_dJ}
\end{align}

\paragraph{$\delta\eta_\xi^\alpha$ terms:}
Pushing forward via $dv = J_\xi\,dV_\xi$:
\begin{align}
  \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
    \mu_\xi^\alpha\,J_\xi\,\delta\eta_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi
  &= \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
    \mu_\xi^\alpha\,\delta\eta_\xi^\alpha \, \dv.
  \label{eq:C2_deta_push}
\end{align}

\paragraph{$\delta\phi_\xi$ terms:}
Pushing forward via $dv = J_\xi\,dV_\xi$:
\begin{align}
  \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
    \mu_\xi^\alpha\,\frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi^2\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}\,\delta\phi_\xi \, dV_\xi
  &= \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
    \frac{\mu_\xi^\alpha}{J_\xi}\,\frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi^2\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}\,\delta\phi_\xi \, \dv.
  \label{eq:C2_dphi_push}
\end{align}

\paragraph{$\delta\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha$ terms:}
Pushing forward:
\begin{align}
  \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
    \mu_\xi^\alpha\,\frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi(\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha)^2}\,\delta\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi
  &= \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
    \frac{\mu_\xi^\alpha}{J_\xi}\,\frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi(\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha)^2}\,\delta\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha \, \dv.
  \label{eq:C2_drho_push}
\end{align}

\paragraph{$\delta c_\xi^\alpha$ terms:}
Pushing forward:
\begin{align}
  -\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
    \frac{\mu_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}\,\delta c_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi
  &= -\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
    \frac{\mu_\xi^\alpha}{J_\xi\phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}\,\delta c_\xi^\alpha \, \dv.
  \label{eq:C2_dc_push}
\end{align}

\subsection{Variation of constraint $C_3$: partition normalization}
\label{sec:var_C3_app}
% ============================================================

\begin{align}
  \delta C_3
  &= \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \delta\pi_\xi\left(\sum_\alpha\eta_\xi^\alpha - 1\right) dV_\xi
  + \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \pi_\xi\,\delta\eta_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi.
  \label{eq:deltaC3_app}
\end{align}
The first line vanishes on the constraint surface $\sum_\alpha\eta_\xi^\alpha = 1$ and recovers it as an Euler--Lagrange equation.  The second line contributes $\pi_\xi$ to each $\delta\eta_\xi^\alpha$ Euler--Lagrange equation.

\subsection{Variation of constraint $C_4$: global mass conservation}
\label{sec:var_C4_app}
% ============================================================

The constraint $C_4$ involves the time derivative $\dot{c}_\xi^\alpha$.  Writing
\begin{equation}
  C_4 = \int_{\mc{B}} \tau\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \frac{\dot{c}^\alpha_\xi}{J_\xi} \,\dv
  = \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}} \tau\,\dot{c}^\alpha_\xi \, dV_\xi,
\end{equation}
and integrating by parts in time (with $\delta c_\xi^\alpha|_{t_1} = \delta c_\xi^\alpha|_{t_2} = 0$):
\begin{align}
  \int_{t_1}^{t_2}\delta C_4\,\mathrm{d}t
  &= \int_{t_1}^{t_2}\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}} \left[
      \delta\tau\,\dot{c}^\alpha_\xi + \tau\,\delta\dot{c}^\alpha_\xi
    \right] dV_\xi\,\mathrm{d}t \notag \\
  &= \int_{t_1}^{t_2}\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}} \left[
      \delta\tau\,\dot{c}^\alpha_\xi - \dot\tau\,\delta c^\alpha_\xi
    \right] dV_\xi\,\mathrm{d}t.
  \label{eq:deltaC4_ibp}
\end{align}
Pushing forward to $\mc{B}$ via $dv = J_\xi\,dV_\xi$:
\begin{align}
  \int_{t_1}^{t_2}\delta C_4\,\mathrm{d}t
  &= \int_{t_1}^{t_2}\left[
    \int_{\mc{B}} \delta\tau\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \frac{\dot{c}^\alpha_\xi}{J_\xi} \,\dv
    - \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}}
        \frac{1}{J_\xi}\frac{D_\xi\tau}{Dt}\,\delta c^\alpha_\xi \,\dv
  \right]\mathrm{d}t.
  \label{eq:deltaC4_app}
\end{align}

\subsection{Collected variational principle}
\label{sec:collected_var}
% ============================================================

Evaluating all variations and collecting by independent variations $\delta\mbf{x}_\xi$, $\delta\phi_\xi$, $\delta\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi$, $\delta\eta^\alpha_\xi$, $\delta\theta^\alpha_\xi$, and $\delta c^\alpha_\xi$ gives
\begin{align}
  0 = &\sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
    -\rho_\xi\mbf{a}_\xi + \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\cdot(\bs\sigma'_\xi)^T + \rho_\xi\mbf{g} + \mbf{b}_\xi
    - \sum_\alpha\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\!\left(\mu_\xi^\alpha\eta_\xi^\alpha\right)
    + \sum_\alpha c^{+\alpha}_\xi\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\tau
  \Bigg]\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_\xi \,\dv \notag \\
  &+ \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_s \,\dv
   - \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_f \,\dv
   - \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\, \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g \cdot \delta\mbf{x}_g \,\dv \notag \\
  &+ \int_{\mc{B}} \left[
      -p_c\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\,\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_f
      + p_c\frac{\phi_f}{\phi}\,\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g\cdot\delta\mbf{x}_g
    \right] \dv \notag \\
  &+ \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
    \lambda
    + \sum_\alpha \frac{\mu_\xi^\alpha}{J_\xi}\frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi^2\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}
  \Bigg] \delta\phi_\xi \,\dv \notag \\
  &+ \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\,\delta\phi_f \,\dv
   + \int_{\mc{B}} P^\star_\phi\,\delta\phi_g \,\dv \notag \\
  &+ \int_{\mc{B}} \left[
      -p_c\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\,\delta\phi_f
      + p_c\frac{\phi_f}{\phi}\,\delta\phi_g
    \right] \dv \notag \\
  &+ \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
    \rho_\xi\left(\sum_\gamma \left.\p{\psi^\gamma_\xi}{\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi}\right\vert\right)
    + \frac{\mu_\xi^\alpha}{J_\xi}\frac{\phi_{\xi0}\bar\rho_{\xi0}^\alpha + c_\xi^\alpha}{\phi_\xi(\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha)^2}
  \Bigg] \delta\bar\rho^\alpha_\xi \,\dv \notag \\
  &+ \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
    \rho_\xi\left(\sum_\gamma \left.\p{\psi^\gamma_\xi}{\eta^\alpha_\xi}\right\vert\right)
    + \mu_\xi^\alpha
    + \pi_\xi
  \Bigg] \delta\eta^\alpha_\xi \,\dv \notag \\
  &+ \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
    \rho_\xi\left(\sum_\gamma \left.\p{\psi^\gamma_\xi}{\theta^\alpha_\xi}\right\vert\right)
  \Bigg] \delta\theta^\alpha_\xi \,\dv \notag \\
  &+ \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}} \Bigg[
    \tfrac{1}{2}\mbf{v}_\xi\cdot\mbf{v}_\xi + \mc{L}^\alpha_\xi
    - \frac{D_\xi\tau}{Dt}
    - \frac{\mu_\xi^\alpha}{J_\xi\phi_\xi\bar\rho_\xi^\alpha}
  \Bigg] \frac{\delta c^\alpha_\xi}{J_\xi} \,\dv \notag \\
  &+ \int_{\mc{B}} \delta\lambda\left(\sum_\xi\phi_\xi - 1\right) \dv \notag \\
  &+ \sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \delta\mu_\xi^\alpha\,h_\xi^\alpha \, dV_\xi \notag \\
  &+ \sum_\xi \int_{\mc{B}_{\xi0}}
      \delta\pi_\xi\left(\sum_\alpha\eta_\xi^\alpha - 1\right) dV_\xi \notag \\
  &+ \int_{\mc{B}} \delta\tau\sum_\xi\sum_\alpha \frac{\dot{c}^\alpha_\xi}{J_\xi} \,\dv.
  \label{eq:collected_full}
\end{align}
Here $c_\xi^{+\alpha} = \dot{c}_\xi^\alpha/J_\xi$ is the mass production rate per unit current volume; the effective Cauchy stress $\bs\sigma'_\xi$ is defined in~\eqref{eq:var_cauchy_def}; $P^\star_\phi$ is defined in~\eqref{eq:Pstar_phi}; and the last four lines recover the constraints~\eqref{eq:C1_def}--\eqref{eq:C4_def}.  Since all variations are independent and arbitrary, each bracketed integrand must vanish pointwise, yielding the Euler--Lagrange equations.

\subsection{Euler--Lagrange equations}

Since the variations are independent and arbitrary, each coefficient in~\eqref{eq:collected_full} must vanish.

\paragraph{Pressure identifications.}  From the coefficient of $\delta\bar\rho_\xi$ we identify the thermodynamic phase pressures
\begin{equation}
  \bar{p}_\xi \equiv \bar\rho_\xi^2\frac{\partial\psi_\xi}{\partial\bar\rho_\xi}, \qquad \xi \in \{f,g\},
  \label{eq:pbar_def}
\end{equation}
so that
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\mu_f J_f}{\phi_f} = \bar p_f, \qquad \frac{\mu_g J_g}{\phi_g} = \bar p_g.
  \label{eq:mu}
\end{equation}
From $\delta\phi_A$ and $\delta\phi_B$,
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\mu_A J_s}{\phi_A} = \lambda, \qquad \frac{\mu_B J_s}{\phi_B} = \lambda.
\end{equation}
From $\delta\phi_f$ and $\delta\phi_g$, including the $P^\star_\phi$ and capillary contributions,
\begin{align}
  \bar p_f &= \lambda - P^\star_\phi - p_c(1-S_f),
  \label{eq:pf}
  \\
  \bar p_g &= \lambda - P^\star_\phi + p_c S_f.
  \label{eq:pg}
\end{align}
Therefore
\begin{equation}
  \lambda = S_f\bar p_f + (1-S_f)\bar p_g + P^\star_\phi.
  \label{eq:lambda}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Fluid momentum.}  From the coefficient of $\delta\mbf{x}_f$,
\begin{equation}
  \rho_f\mbf{a}_f = \rho_f\mbf{g} + \mbf{b}_f - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}(\mu_f J_f) + \lambda\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f - P^\star_\phi\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f - p_c\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f + c^+_f\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\tau.
  \label{eq:mom_fluid_raw}
\end{equation}
Using $\mu_f J_f = \phi_f \bar p_f$ and \eqref{eq:pf},
\begin{align*}
  -\nabla_{\mbf{x}}(\phi_f \bar p_f) + \lambda\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f - P^\star_\phi\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f - p_c\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f
  &= -\phi_f\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\bar p_f - \bar p_f\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f \\
  &\quad + \left(\lambda - P^\star_\phi - p_c\frac{\phi_g}{\phi}\right)\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_f \\
  &= -\phi_f\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\bar p_f.
\end{align*}
Hence the fluid momentum balance reduces to the phase-pressure form
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\rho_f\mbf{a}_f = \rho_f\mbf{g} + \mbf{b}_f - \phi_f\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\bar p_f + c^+_f\left(\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\tau - \mbf{v}_f\right),}
  \label{eq:mom_fluid}
\end{equation}
where the reaction-momentum term has been written in the standard Bedford--Drumheller form.

\paragraph{Gas momentum.}  From the coefficient of $\delta\mbf{x}_g$,
\begin{equation}
  \rho_g\mbf{a}_g = \rho_g\mbf{g} + \mbf{b}_g - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}(\mu_g J_g) + \lambda\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g - P^\star_\phi\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g + p_c\frac{\phi_f}{\phi}\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g + c^+_g\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\tau.
  \label{eq:mom_gas_raw}
\end{equation}
Using $\mu_g J_g = \phi_g \bar p_g$ and \eqref{eq:pg},
\begin{align*}
  -\nabla_{\mbf{x}}(\phi_g \bar p_g) + \lambda\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g - P^\star_\phi\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g + p_c\frac{\phi_f}{\phi}\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g
  &= -\phi_g\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\bar p_g - \bar p_g\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g \\
  &\quad + \left(\lambda - P^\star_\phi + p_c\frac{\phi_f}{\phi}\right)\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\phi_g \\
  &= -\phi_g\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\bar p_g.
\end{align*}
Hence the gas momentum balance reduces to
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\rho_g\mbf{a}_g = \rho_g\mbf{g} + \mbf{b}_g - \phi_g\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\bar p_g + c^+_g\left(\nabla_{\mbf{x}}\tau - \mbf{v}_g\right).}
  \label{eq:mom_gas}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Overall momentum.}  Summing the solid, fluid, and gas balances gives the mixture equation.  The direct inter-component body-force pair terms from~\eqref{eq:phi_variation_split} cancel in the sum, but the porosity-conjugate contribution survives through the solid pressure algebra.  Writing the weighted phase pressure as $\bar p \equiv S_f\bar p_f + (1-S_f)\bar p_g$, the overall balance takes the form
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\rho\mbf{a} = \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\cdot(\bs\sigma'_s)^T - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\bar p - \nabla_{\mbf{x}}\left[(1-\phi)P^\star_\phi\right] + \rho\mbf{g} - c^+_f\left(\mbf{v}_f - \mbf{v}_s\right) - c^+_g\left(\mbf{v}_g - \mbf{v}_s\right).}
  \label{eq:mom_overall}
\end{equation}
When $\psi_\xi$ does not depend on $\phi$ ($P^\star_\phi = 0$), this reduces to the standard mixture form.

\paragraph{Evolution of $\tau$.}  From the coefficient of $\delta c_\xi$ and choosing to evaluate the functions for the reactant $A$,
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\left.\p{\tau}{t}\right|_{\mbf{X}_s} = \frac{1}{2}\mbf{v}_s\cdot\mbf{v}_s - \mc{L}_A - \frac{\lambda}{\bar\rho_A}.}
  \label{eq:tau_final}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Connection to the Coleman--Noll procedure}
\label{sec:coleman_connection}

The variational derivation and the Coleman--Noll procedure are complementary.  Hamilton's principle takes the free energies (e.g.\ $\psi_\xi(\mbf{F}_s, \phi)$) as functions of \emph{independent fields} (subject to constraints) and derives the momentum balances, pressure identifications, and body forces as Euler--Lagrange equations.  The Coleman--Noll procedure (Appendix~\ref{sec:coleman}) starts from the entropy inequality with the \emph{same} energy and derives constitutive restrictions.  The multiplicative decomposition $\mbf{F}_s = \mbf{A}_s \bar{\mbf{F}}_s$ is a constitutive choice imposed within the Coleman--Noll procedure, giving $\psi_\xi(\mbf{F}_s, \phi) = \psi^{\text{iso}}_\xi(\bar{\mbf{F}}_s) + \psi^{\text{dis}}_\xi(J_s, \phi)$, which is a specialization of the general $\psi_\xi(\mbf{F}_s, \phi)$ used in the variational principle.
